DISASTER RESPONSE:
- Follow Drop, Cover, and Hold during earthquakes
- Stay away from windows, heavy objects, and tall furniture
- Have an emergency kit ready (water, food, flashlight, radio, first aid)
- Know your evacuation routes and meeting points
- Listen to official announcements from authorities

HAZARD INFORMATION:
- Earthquake magnitude scale: 1-3 (minor), 4-5 (light), 6-7 (strong), 8+ (great)
- Volcanic alert levels: 0 (normal), 1 (abnormal), 2 (increasing unrest), 3 (high unrest), 4 (hazardous eruption imminent), 5 (hazardous eruption ongoing)
- Tsunami warnings are issued by PHIVOLCS-DOST
- Stay informed through official channels and verified news sources
